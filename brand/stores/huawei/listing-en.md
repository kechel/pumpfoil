## Full introduction (max. 8000)
Pumpfoil turns your HUAWEI WATCH into a recorder for pump foiling. Start a session, go pumping, and the watch records GPS, motion and heart rate. Afterwards your session is analysed on pumpfoil.org: every run on the foil, distance, speed, pumping cadence, glide phases and your progress over time.

ON THE WATCH
• One tap to start, hold to stop or pause – safe with wet hands
• Live data pages: speed, distance, time on foil, last run, number of runs, heart rate, clock
• Your own data page layouts, designed on pumpfoil.org and sent to the watch
• Keeps recording with the screen off
• No phone app needed: the watch uploads directly to pumpfoil.org
• Recordings are kept on the watch until the server has confirmed every part – nothing gets lost if the connection drops

GETTING STARTED
1. Create a free account at pumpfoil.org
2. Open Pumpfoil on the watch and tap "pair at pumpfoil.org" to show a code
3. Enter the code in your pumpfoil.org account – done

You can record before pairing; the sessions are uploaded as soon as the watch is paired.

ON PUMPFOIL.ORG
• Map of every session with each run highlighted
• Runs, longest run, top speed, pumps and cadence
• Progress charts and personal records
• Spots, equipment (foil, board, stabiliser) and a community of pump foilers

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your recordings are only used to analyse your sessions on pumpfoil.org. You can delete individual sessions or your whole account at any time.

Pumpfoil is made by pump foilers for pump foilers. Have fun, keep pumping!
