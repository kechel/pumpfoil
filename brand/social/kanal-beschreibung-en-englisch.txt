Track Every Pump

Turn your sports watch into a pumpfoil computer: GPS track, foiling distance, pump cadence and glides – fully automatic from every session.

On your watch & phone
Apps for Garmin, Apple Watch, Wear OS and Amazfit – plus native apps for iOS & Android with a recorder that works without a watch. Link your account: Polar, Suunto, COROS, Xiaomi (via Mi Fitness).

What you get
• Configurable data fields: speed, heart rate, distance, time
• Vibration alarm above/below your target speed (from foil and weight)
• Carve detection with highlights on the map of every run
• Stats per foil: distance, longest run, heart rate – which one carries you further?
• Season history, recording works offline, upload happens by itself

Community
Share and compare sessions, community records and leaderboards per spot – and every spot gets its own chat group automatically.

Open source
Free software under AGPL-3.0, in 17 languages.
